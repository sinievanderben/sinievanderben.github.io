Research

I am a PhD student in mechanistic interpretability, working on how models store and organize what they know. My research asks where knowledge lives inside a model, how it can be read out and edited, and whether representations are consistent and universal across architectures and training runs.

A central interest is how individual features relate to one another: whether they compose, form hierarchies, and connect into the circuits that drive a model's behavior. I am exploring techniques to move from describing what a model represents toward predicting what it will compute.

I also have a general interest in health and medicine, so if you have a strong interpretability project in these directions, I’d love to hear about it.

  • Circuit discovery and explanation
  • Representation readout, editing, and universality
  • Feature composition, hierarchies, and behavioral circuits
